## Illustration Style

**Preferred model:** ReCraft V3
**Preferred style:** `digital_illustration/hand_drawing`
**Runner-up styles:** `digital_illustration/infantile_sketch`, `digital_illustration/2d_art_poster`

### Style Principles
- Hand-drawn, textured crayon/chalk/watercolor feel
- White or cream backgrounds with at least 40% open space
- Full rainbow color diversity — every element a different color
- Loose, imperfect line work — not clean vector art
- Warm, playful, joyful, human mood
- No dark backgrounds, no corporate/generic aesthetic

### Key Visual Motifs
- **Magnifying glass** — represents AI matching (core product)
- **Hummingbird with envelope** — connection between makers
- **Lightbulb / lightbulb-sail boats** — ideas and creativity
- **Makers at tables** — collaboration, building together
- **Four-pointed stars / sparkles** — scattered sparingly as accents
