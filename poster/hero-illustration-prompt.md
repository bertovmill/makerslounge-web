# MakersLounge Hero Illustration - Image Generation Prompt

## Prompt (v2 — Simplified & Colorful)

(Full version for Midjourney/DALL-E — no character limit)

A whimsical hand-drawn illustration in a storybook editorial style with a rich, diverse color palette — sunny yellow, sky blue, leafy green, warm orange, soft pink, lavender, and pops of red. The scene is open and breathable with generous white space on a cream/off-white background.

**Left side:** A single maker at a drafting table, sketching something that comes to life off the page. A gentle winding river flows beneath, carrying one or two small boats with lightbulb sails. Keep it simple — just the maker, the table, and the river.

**Center:** A friendly magnifying glass floating in open space, its lens showing constellation lines connecting people — representing AI matching. Two hands reaching toward each other inside the lens. Rays of warm light emanate outward. Plenty of breathing room around it for headline text to sit over.

**Right side:** Three or four makers sitting around a round table, each doing something different — one on a laptop, one sculpting, one sketching. A hummingbird carrying a small envelope flies between the left and right groups. Keep the group loose and casual.

**Scattered sparingly:** A few four-pointed stars, a couple of small sparkle marks, one or two floating music notes. Do NOT overcrowd — leave at least 40% of the composition as open white/cream space. No winding roads, no cityscape, no hills. Let the three focal points (maker, magnifying glass, group table) carry the scene.

**Style details:** Hand-drawn ink outlines with visible texture, filled with soft watercolor washes. Use the FULL rainbow — each element a different color so the illustration feels joyful and diverse. Sunny yellows, ocean blues, forest greens, warm oranges, blush pinks, rich purples, bright reds — all in soft watercolor tones so nothing clashes. The line work should feel loose and confident, like a talented illustrator's sketchbook. Paper grain texture visible. The mood is warm, playful, and inviting.

**Dimensions:** Ultra-wide panoramic format (roughly 3:1 ratio), designed to wrap around centered text. The center must be sparse enough for large headline text to overlay cleanly.

## Prompt (v3 — ReCraft, under 1000 characters)

Style: `digital_illustration/urban_sketching` | Wide aspect ratio (1820x1024)

Colors: none (remove all color params)

Minimalist illustration on a pure white background, not colored or tinted. Mostly empty white space with very few small, simple elements scattered loosely. A tiny sailboat with a lightbulb sail. A small magnifying glass. Two people sitting at a table sketching together. A little hummingbird carrying an envelope. A few tiny stars. That is all. The elements are very small compared to the canvas. At least 70% of the image is pure untouched white. Think minimalist editorial illustration with lots of breathing room. Simple, sparse, joyful. Less is more.

## Changes from v1

- **Simplified composition:** 3 focal points instead of a dense landscape
- **40%+ white space:** Breathing room, not wall-to-wall detail
- **Full rainbow palette:** Diverse, joyful colors instead of limited coral/teal
- **Removed:** Winding roads, cityscapes, hills, cobblestone, gears, paint splatters, circuit traces, balloons, potted plants, clouds
- **Kept:** Magnifying glass (AI matching), hummingbird (connection), makers collaborating, lightbulb boats (ideas)
